I think ice cream is an excellent first topic.

Not because it is the largest category, but because it satisfies almost every requirement for SEO:

High seasonal search volume (as your Google Trends link shows, searches surge every summer).
Strong consumer interest ("Which ice cream is healthiest?").
Large enough category for meaningful comparisons.
Lots of media potential.
Highly shareable on social media.

However, I would not make the article about sugar, saturated fat and additives alone.

That would look like hundreds of existing nutrition articles.

Instead, use what almost nobody else has:

You have the entire Latvian product database.

The article should answer a question no one else can.

I would make it data-driven

Instead of

"Sugar in ice cream"

make it

Analysis of 146 large ice creams sold in Latvia

or

Which ice creams sold in Latvia are actually the healthiest?

Immediately the article becomes original.

Structure
1. Executive summary

Start with something journalists could quote.

Example:

We analysed 146 large-pack ice creams available in Latvia.

The difference between products is enormous:

sugar varies almost 3×
saturated fat varies 4×
additive count ranges from 0 to more than 10
only X% receive Nutri-Score A or B
X% are NOVA 4

Five bullet points.

Many readers won't continue, but they'll remember these.
